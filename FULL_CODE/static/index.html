How Voice of Store Engine Works

From raw, unstructured customer sentiment to on-the-ground operational resolution in four automated steps.

01

Collect Feedback

Customer ratings, app submissions, staff shift notes, and verified customer observations are ingested in real time across the retail network.

Stage 1 • Ingestion
02

Classify & Organize

The machine learning engine parses text to determine specific issue categories, assigns functional owners, and computes severity and actionability scores.

Stage 2 • AI Triage
03

Identify Recurring Issues

The emerging-issues radar detects statistical spikes and localized hazards, alerting store managers with direct customer quote evidence.

Stage 3 • Spike Detection
04

Review & Resolve

Store managers execute corrective actions, enter resolution notes, and update incident statuses in an immutable audit log visible to customers.

Stage 4 • Closed-Loop Action
